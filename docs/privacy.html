Privacy policy

Datenschutzerklärung under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Last updated 5 October 2026.

Short version

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it loads nothing from third-party servers. The web server keeps a standard access log for 14 days. The RefRef app does not send your images or any usage data anywhere.

Server log files

Each time you load a page, the web server automatically records:

  • your IP address
  • date and time of the request
  • the page or file requested, the HTTP status code and the amount of data sent
  • the referring page, if your browser sends it
  • your browser and operating system (user agent)

This is needed to deliver the website and to detect and fix errors and attacks. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in running a secure website). The logs are deleted automatically after 14 days. They are not combined with other data or used to identify visitors.

Hosting

The website runs on a server rented from Hostinger International Ltd., 61 Lordou Vironos Street, 6023 Larnaca, Cyprus. The server is located in Germany. Hostinger processes data on my behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR).

Live demo

The demo on the home page runs entirely in your browser. Images you drop or paste into it are not uploaded and are not stored. They are gone when you close or reload the page.

Downloads and external links

The download buttons and the GitHub link lead to GitHub, operated by GitHub, Inc., USA. The support buttons lead to Ko-fi, operated by Ko-fi Labs Limited, UK. Clicking one of these links takes you to their website, and their own privacy policies apply. No data is sent to them unless you click.

The RefRef app

The desktop app has no account, telemetry or update check. Boards are saved only where you choose on your computer. The app connects to the internet only when you drag an image from a website into it, to download that image.

Email

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. I delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.
